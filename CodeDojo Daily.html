ABOUT THE APP

Small daily practice. Stronger coding habits.

CodeDojo Daily turns algorithm practice into a routine with bite-sized challenges, a personal progress tracker, and a little friendly competition.

🥋 Daily challenges

Try a rotating coding problem, work through examples, and use public tests before submitting against the full test set.

💻 Practice your way

Browse problems by topic and difficulty, then solve them in the workspace with JavaScript or Python.

📈 Track your progress

Build a streak, earn points and belts, and review your solved problems and activity history on your profile.

🤝 Learn together

Create or join a demo squad and follow its score on the leaderboard while you practice.

✨ Local AI Coach

Get curated hints, a problem explanation, and feedback on test results. The coach is rule-based and does not send code to an AI service.

CodeDojo Daily is a browser-based demo. Progress and drafts are stored in this browser; sign-in, squads, and leaderboard activity are not backed by shared online accounts or servers.
